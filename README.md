## 🔭 About me

I build and benchmark machine learning systems, from research prototypes to production-ready code. My recent work covers:

- 🧬 **Medical AI**: rare disease diagnosis from craniofacial images and phenotypes (Institut Imagine), and disease and readmission prediction across 280+ conditions (UNIL Applied AI Lab).
- 🧠 **LLM systems**: reasoning-data distillation, LoRA/QLoRA/full fine-tuning, GRPO and reward modelling (LiGHT, EPFL–Harvard).
- 🤖 **AI agents**: RAG pipelines and agent platforms, plus studies of how AI agents behave and interact.
- 🚀 **Leadership**: previously CTO of an AI-agent startup, leading a team of 15 engineers.
